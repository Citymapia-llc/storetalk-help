Every sign-in code is a real WhatsApp message that Meta charges to your account. A public sign-in page that
sends messages to any number is an attractive target, so StoreTalk applies several layers of protection
before any code is sent.

## Protections

| Protection | What it does |
|------------|--------------|
| **Bot check** | A Cloudflare check on the number step, usually invisible to real people. |
| **Per-number limits** | Stops one number being flooded with codes. |
| **Per-connection limits** | Stops one device or network requesting codes for many numbers. |
| **Daily limit per website** | Your *Codes per day* setting. Once reached, new codes wait until the next day (UTC). |
| **Daily limit per business** | All your websites together can send at most 5× your largest *Codes per day*. |
| **Allowed countries** | Numbers from other countries are refused before any message is sent. |
| **Wrong-code lockout** | Too many wrong codes pause the number for the rest of the day. |
| **Exact redirect addresses** | Customers only return to addresses you registered; a wrong address shows an error instead of redirecting. |
| **PKCE on every sign-in** | A one-time check that stops an intercepted code from being used by anyone else. |
| **Single-use codes** | Sign-in codes expire after 5 minutes; the code your website exchanges expires after 60 seconds. |

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Codes per number | 3 per hour, 10 per day |
| Codes per connection (IP address) | 10 per hour, 30 per day |
| Codes per website | Your *Codes per day* setting (default 200, up to 5,000) |
| Codes per business | 5 × your largest *Codes per day* |
| Wrong codes per number | 5 per code; 15 per day before the number is paused until the next day (UTC) |
| Code lifetime | 5 minutes |
| Resend | After 30 seconds |
| *Continue as* | Your *Stay signed in* setting (default 30 days, up to 90), extended on each use |

When a limit is hit, the customer sees *“Too many code requests. Please wait a while and try again.”* The
**Blocked** count in Settings shows how often this happens.

## Your data and your customers' data

- Codes are stored only as a one-way fingerprint and deleted once used or expired.
- Your website receives only what the customer agreed to share.
- The sign-in ID (`sub`) is different for every business, so two businesses can't match up a customer by it.
- Each customer is added to your Contacts by their WhatsApp number. Signing in never subscribes anyone to
  marketing or changes an opt-out.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my business need its own WhatsApp Business account?">
    The code has to come from a number your customers recognise as yours, and Meta bills those messages to
    the account that sends them. Using the shared StoreTalk number would show StoreTalk instead of your
    business. Connect your own account in **Settings → WhatsApp**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does StoreTalk charge for each sign-in?">
    No. Meta charges your WhatsApp account its authentication-message rate for each code. Customers who use
    *Continue as* don't get a code, so those sign-ins are free.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customers say they didn't get a code">
    Check that:
    - Settings shows **Ready — codes are sent from your WhatsApp number** (not *Waiting for Meta*).
    - Your WhatsApp connection in **Settings → WhatsApp** is healthy.
    - The customer entered the right country code and the number uses WhatsApp.
    - You haven't reached your **Codes per day** limit (see **Blocked** in usage).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My website shows “Suspended”">
    Your own WhatsApp Business account isn't connected, or your plan isn't active. Fix that and the website
    resumes automatically on the next sign-in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customers see “This website isn't registered for Sign in with WhatsApp”">
    The client ID or the redirect address your website sends doesn't match Settings. Redirect addresses must
    match exactly — including `https`, `www`, and the path.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can customers from other countries sign in?">
    Only if you add their country's calling code (e.g. `44` for the UK) under **Countries** for that website.
    International authentication messages cost more, so add only the countries you need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I lost the client secret">
    Click **New secret** on the website in Settings and update your website. The old secret keeps working for
    24 hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use it in my iOS or Android app?">
    Yes — add a **Browser or mobile app** client. See [Mobile apps](/sign-in-with-whatsapp/developer-guide#mobile-apps).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A customer changed their phone number">
    A new number is a new sign-in. Ask the customer to sign in with the new number, then link it to their
    existing account on your side (for example after they confirm their old details).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
